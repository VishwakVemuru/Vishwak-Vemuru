Privacy

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it sets no cookies of its own.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to me through Netlify Forms, the service that hosts this site. I use them only to reply to you, and I don't share them with anyone else. To have a message deleted, email me and I'll remove it.

Hosting and fonts
