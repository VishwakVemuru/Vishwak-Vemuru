Privacy

Last updated 5 October 2026.

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it sets no cookies.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to me through Netlify Forms, the service that hosts this site. I use them only to reply to you, and I don't share them with anyone. To have a message deleted, email me and I'll remove it.

Stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers; see Google's privacy policy.
