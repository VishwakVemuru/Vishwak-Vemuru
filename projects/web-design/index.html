Overview

The idea

Alongside the engineering projects, I design and build websites for local businesses: restaurants, design studios, estate agents, gyms and clinics.

The studio site shows ten complete demo templates, two per industry, each a real working page with its own typefaces, colours and booking or enquiry flow.

Features

What it does

  • Ten live demosTwo per industry: restaurants and cafés, interiors and architecture, real estate, fitness and yoga, salons and clinics.
  • Hand-codedPlain HTML and CSS, with no page builders or heavy themes.
  • Mobile firstDesigned for a phone screen, then widened for larger ones.
  • Built to convertBooking forms, menus, price lists and tap-to-call numbers.
  • Yours to keepOne folder of files the business owns and can host anywhere.

Under the hood

How I build them

  1. Each template has its own type pairing and palette, chosen for the business.
  2. Layouts are built around one clear action: book, order, enquire or call.
  3. Pages ship as static files, so they load fast and are simple to host.
