CricScore

A ball-by-ball cricket scorer for real matches with friends: free hits, extras, player stats and undo, all on your phone and offline.

Overview

The idea

CricScore was built to solve a real problem: scoring local cricket matches accurately on a phone, without an internet connection or a complicated app.

Pick a format, name the teams, unlock scoring with the admin PIN and log every delivery. The app handles the rules (extras, free hits, the last batter batting alone) and keeps everyone's career stats up to date.

Features

What it does

  • Ball-by-ball scoringLog every delivery with full detail.
  • Any formatT20, ODI, T10, Test or your own custom rules.
  • Free-hit logicAutomatically flags and enforces free hits after no-balls.
  • ExtrasWides, no-balls, byes and leg-byes handled correctly.
  • UndoStep back through deliveries without corrupting the scorecard.
  • Player career statsBatting and bowling tracked across matches, with a top players board.
  • Joker roleA custom player role with its own in-game logic.
  • Admin PINLocks scoring so nobody edits by accident.
  • Works offlineSaved to localStorage, so a refresh never loses the match.

Under the hood

How it works

  1. Set up the match: format, teams, overs and players.
  2. Enter the admin PIN to unlock scoring.
  3. Tap the result after each delivery.
  4. Player stats update automatically; undo fixes mistakes.
  5. A match summary closes each innings.

The rules engine sits on a history of deliveries, which is what makes undo safe.
