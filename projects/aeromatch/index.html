AeroMatch

An aircraft recommender. Describe the mission (range, passengers, cargo, runway, terrain, budget) and it ranks 123 aircraft and helicopters by how well they fit.

Overview

The idea

Choosing an aircraft is a trade-off: range against payload, runway length against speed, comfort against operating cost. AeroMatch turns that into a question you can answer in a minute.

You describe the mission, from a 3,000 km scheduled route to a ship-deck rescue, and it scores every aircraft and helicopter in a 123-entry database against it. The best fits come back ranked, with a score out of 100 and a plain-English reason for each pick.

Features

What it does

  • Mission basicsRange, passengers, cargo weight and cargo volume.
  • 13 mission typesFrom scheduled airline and cargo to medevac, search and rescue, offshore, heli-skiing and naval.
  • Operational limitsRunway length and surface (paved, unpaved or water), hot-and-high, arctic, short-strip or ETOPS conditions.
  • Helicopter optionsHover capability and landing zones from full helipads to ship decks and unprepared ground.
  • Your prioritiesRank fuel efficiency, comfort, operating cost, range margin and speed in the order that matters to you.
  • Explained resultsTop picks with a score breakdown and a reason, plus a sortable table of every qualifying aircraft.

Under the hood

How it scores

Range fitNeeds the route plus a 10 % fuel reserve; extra range scores higher
Capacity fitSeats, cargo mass and volume, with a penalty for aircraft far too big for the job
Runway & surfaceRunway required against runway available, and paved / unpaved / water
TerrainHot-and-high, cold, short-strip and ETOPS capability
Mission & budgetExact or compatible mission roles, and budget tier
PrioritiesSpeed, efficiency (payload × range) and comfort weighted by your ranking

Each factor scores 0–100; together they give the match score out of 100.
