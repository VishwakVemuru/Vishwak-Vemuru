Overview

The idea

A reference shelf for aircraft: 123 types, from the A220 and 737 MAX to helicopters and seaplanes, each with the numbers that matter when you compare them.

Search by name, manufacturer or category, narrow it down with one tap, and open any card for its spec sheet and a link to read more. The same database powers AeroMatch.

Features

What it does

  • 123 aircraftAirliners, regional jets, turboprops, freighters, business jets, military transports, seaplanes and helicopters.
  • Nine categoriesFilter to narrow-body, wide-body, regional, turboprop and bush, cargo, bizjet and VIP, military, seaplane or helicopter.
  • SearchFind a type by name, manufacturer or category as you type.
  • Spec sheetsRange, seats, cargo, cruise speed, runway needed and maximum take-off weight for every aircraft.
  • Read moreEach aircraft links to its Wikipedia article.
  • Works on phonesThe grid reflows to fit a phone screen.
