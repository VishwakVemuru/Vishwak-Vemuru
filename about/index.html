Who I am

I'm a student with a deep interest in aeronautical engineering and a habit of building things from scratch. Whether it's an aircraft performance tool, an orbital mechanics game or a web app, I like turning ideas into something real that other people can use.

I model in Fusion 360, Blender and TinkerCAD, and I build for the web with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Every project on this site is written from scratch, with no frameworks or physics libraries, so I understand every equation that's running.

Right now I'm exploring pathways into aerospace engineering, and I'm open to opportunities, collaborations and feedback.

What drives me

Three things I keep coming back to

Flight

How lift, drag and thrust decide what an aircraft can do: how far it flies, how fast it climbs, how efficient it is. The Aircraft Performance Calculator came from wanting to play with those numbers directly.

Orbits

Exorbit started as a way to see the orbital physics behind real satellite missions: gravity, orbital velocity and escape velocity, turned into a game you can actually fly.

Real problems

CricScore exists because scoring local cricket matches on a phone was harder than it should be, especially without internet or a complicated app.

How I build

Equations first, then code

Each project starts from the physics: the lift and drag equations, the Breguet range equation, Newton's law of gravitation. Then it becomes something interactive, with sliders, a game loop or a scoreboard, so the maths turns into something you can feel.

I keep the stack small on purpose: vanilla JavaScript, Canvas and the browser. Everything ships as a static site, and everything here is live.
